# Provance Executive Summary

Prepared: 2026-07-29

## Company Overview

Provance is building trust infrastructure for synthetic media verification. Its current documented product direction is image-first verification with a report-first workflow designed for users who need evidence they can review, explain, and share. The company is targeting a gap left by score-only detection tools: real operational trust for high-scrutiny decisions.

## Problem

Synthetic media quality is improving rapidly, while the infrastructure for verifying authenticity remains fragmented. Many tools provide confidence scores or moderation outputs, but high-stakes users often need:

- explainable evidence
- defensible verdicts
- report artifacts
- workflow support for uncertain or inconclusive cases

This matters in journalism, investigations, legal-adjacent review, trust and safety, fraud operations, and enterprise risk workflows.

## Product And Technology

Based on the current documentation, Provance has a real product and technical foundation already in place:

- React/Vite frontend
- NestJS backend
- Supabase-backed auth, database, and storage
- queue-backed processing path
- uploads, reports, account, and admin surfaces
- waitlist and invite-based onboarding

The product is beyond concept stage, but it is not yet enterprise-ready. The docs still identify open work in session hardening, observability, queue reliability, benchmark publication, and commercialization of billing and APIs.

## Why It Matters

Provance is not trying to win by being “another detector.” Its strongest documented differentiation is an evidence-first workflow that can sit between provenance systems, API-led detection vendors, and enterprise security platforms. If successful, it could become a trusted verification layer for decisions where media authenticity changes outcomes.

## Market Opportunity

Provance operates at the intersection of AI verification, digital forensics, trust and safety, fraud prevention, and evidence-sensitive workflow software. The near-term market is best framed as the set of workflows where authenticity materially affects editorial, legal, investigative, compliance, or enterprise decisions. That is a credible and valuable niche with expansion potential.

## Business Model

The internal commercial model is SaaS plus API:

- Trial
- Pro
- Team
- Enterprise
- API Lite
- API Pro

This is a strong monetization structure conceptually, but revenue validation is still ahead.

## Investment Readiness

Current diligence posture: strong pre-seed candidate, not yet institutional seed-ready.

Documented strengths:

- differentiated trust thesis
- real product and architecture foundation
- disciplined roadmap and fundraising posture
- credible long-term moat concept around reports, methodology, and data

Current gaps:

- no documented recurring revenue
- no published benchmark results
- no documented signed design partners
- incomplete enterprise controls and security hardening

## Valuation View

A grounded current financing posture is:

- immediate raise: `$500K-$750K`
- preferred ask: around `$650K`
- fair current SAFE cap range: `$5M-$7M` post-money

Upside to a stronger seed valuation depends on benchmark credibility, paid pilot conversion, and clear enterprise-readiness progress.

## Recommendation

Current rating: `Watch`

Provance is investable today for specialist pre-seed investors who are comfortable underwriting category formation and execution risk. It is not yet ready for broad seed-market conviction. The highest-value next milestones are:

1. complete the image-first report artifact
2. publish benchmark and methodology credibility assets
3. secure 3-5 credible design partners
4. convert early pilots into paid usage
5. harden security and operational posture

## Bottom Line

Provance has the ingredients of a credible digital-trust startup: a real problem, a differentiated workflow position, a functioning technical base, and a disciplined view of what has not yet been proven. The opportunity is meaningful, but the company’s next step is proof, not expansion.
