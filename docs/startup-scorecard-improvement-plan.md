## 4. Why Audio And Video Help

The current documentation still describes video and audio verification as deferred. `Documented fact`

So adding them now would represent a strategic expansion, not a reflection of current shipped state. `Documented fact`

If done well, audio and video help in five major ways.

### 1. Bigger Market Story

Provance stops looking like a narrow image-verification tool and starts looking like a broader media-verification platform.

This improves:

- market opportunity
- revenue potential
- long-term vision

### 2. Better Enterprise Relevance

Enterprise buyers often care about more than still images. They care about:

- manipulated clips
- synthetic voice
- short-form video evidence
- cross-format trust workflows

That helps:

- enterprise readiness
- business model quality
- customer acquisition

### 3. Stronger Competitive Positioning

A controlled multimodal MVP makes Provance more comparable to higher-end trust and deepfake vendors while keeping its report-first differentiation.

That helps:

- innovation
- competitive advantage
- overall investment quality

### 4. Better Expansion Revenue

Multiformat verification creates a cleaner path toward:

- usage-based pricing
- premium forensic reports
- team workflows
- enterprise contracts
- future API monetization

### 5. Stronger Platform Narrative

Investors generally assign more strategic value to a platform path than to a single-format point solution, assuming the execution remains credible.

## 5. Where Audio And Video Can Hurt

Multimodal expansion only helps if quality stays high.

If handled badly, it will reduce confidence instead of improving it.

### Main Risks

1. execution risk rises because three media pipelines are harder than one
2. technical risk rises because latency, storage, and compute costs increase
3. benchmarking becomes harder because each format needs its own evaluation discipline
4. trust risk rises because false positives in audio and video can be more damaging
5. roadmap discipline can weaken if the image wedge is not finished first

The wrong move would be launching image, video, and audio all at once with weak reports and weak benchmarks. That would likely lower the quality of the company in investor eyes even if the surface area looked bigger. `Recommendation / informed assumption`

## 6. Best Multimodal MVP Strategy

The strongest path is:

- keep the core positioning as `image-first`
- add `audio beta` and `video beta` as controlled extensions
- use one common evidence and report framework across all three formats
- avoid claiming full multimodal maturity until results are benchmarked and defensible

### Recommended Product Positioning

Provance should describe itself as:

`An evidence-first media verification platform, starting with image verification and expanding into controlled audio and video verification workflows.`

That positioning is stronger than either extreme:

- weaker than needed: "image-only detector"
- too aggressive: "fully mature multimodal verification platform"

## 7. Recommended MVP Scope For Image, Audio, And Video

### Image

This should remain the strongest and most mature format.

Must-have improvements:

- stronger verdict and uncertainty language
- cleaner evidence presentation
- exportable / shareable report artifact
- benchmark and methodology proof

### Audio Beta

Audio is the safest second format if kept narrow.

Recommended scope:

- uploaded speech files only
- authenticity / synthetic voice risk scoring
- metadata and waveform-oriented evidence blocks
- limited file duration caps
- report output aligned to the image report structure

Why audio second:

- strong demand in fraud, social engineering, and trust workflows
- narrower input profile than broad video
- lower complexity than full video verification

### Video Beta

Video should begin with a limited, controlled scope.

Recommended scope:

- short uploaded clips only
- capped duration and file size
- asynchronous processing only
- evidence blocks broken into frame, sequence, metadata, and audio-track sections where available
- report output that explains uncertainty clearly

Why video later:

- it has the biggest TAM signal
- it is also the highest cost and highest complexity format
- benchmark quality is harder to establish

## 9. 3-Month, 6-Month, And 12-Month Plan

### 0-3 Months

Primary objective: increase confidence without losing focus.

Priority actions:

1. finish the image report artifact
2. publish a benchmark and methodology plan for image verification
3. harden sessions, admin posture, and observability
4. define the shared report schema for image, audio, and video
5. design the controlled beta specs for audio and video

Expected impact:

- improves product
- improves technology
- improves security
- improves enterprise readiness

### 3-6 Months

Primary objective: turn the image wedge into proof and add one narrow new format.

Priority actions:

1. secure 3-5 design partners
2. publish initial image benchmark results
3. launch audio beta with strict file limits and report outputs
4. begin paid pilot conversations around image plus audio workflows
5. produce external-facing trust collateral: methodology summary, security summary, sample reports

Expected impact:

- improves customer acquisition
- improves business model credibility
- improves AI readiness
- improves overall investment quality

### 6-12 Months

Primary objective: become a real media-verification platform without overextending.

Priority actions:

1. launch controlled short-form video beta
2. convert early pilots into paid recurring customers
3. tighten multimodal benchmark and evaluation discipline
4. build clearer enterprise packaging around report workflows and team use cases
5. prepare the API roadmap once image and multimodal beta outputs are operationally stable

Expected impact:

- improves revenue potential
- improves competitive advantage
- improves scalability
- supports a stronger seed narrative

## 10. What Investors Will Like Most

The strongest investor reaction will come from this sequence:

1. image proof
2. paid pilot conversion
3. security and enterprise hardening
4. controlled audio and video beta

That sequence says:

- the product works
- customers care
- the platform can be trusted
- the market can expand

That is much stronger than trying to impress investors with breadth before Provance has enough proof. `Recommendation / informed assumption`

## 11. Final Recommendation

If Provance wants better scorecard numbers all around, the winning strategy is:

`Proof first, paid demand second, multimodal expansion third.`

The company **should** move toward image, audio, and video verification inside the MVP period, but in this order:

1. make image excellent
2. add audio in controlled beta
3. add short-form video in controlled beta
4. keep one shared report system across all formats
5. benchmark each format before making bigger claims

### Bottom Line

Adding audio and video can materially improve Provance's startup scorecard, valuation narrative, and investor attractiveness.

But the lift comes from **credible multimodal execution**, not from adding more boxes to the product surface.

If executed with discipline, Provance can move from a promising image-first pre-seed company to a much stronger media-verification platform with a more compelling seed story.
